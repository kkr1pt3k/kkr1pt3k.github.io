01

LightCSPNet — Cloud Detection & Segmentation in Landsat 8 Imagery

2026 · Independent research · Python, PyTorch

A cloud-segmentation pipeline for Landsat 8 scenes. The work moved through three stages: raw scenes from USGS EarthExplorer, a hand-assembled experimental dataset with Otsu / brightness pseudo-masks, and finally supervised training and evaluation on the annotated 38-Cloud benchmark.

LightCSPNet is a compact encoder–decoder: convolutional blocks, progressive downsampling, bilinear upsampling, a high-resolution skip connection, and weighted binary cross-entropy to handle heavy cloud/background imbalance.

02

MCQ Marking Software

MATLAB · Image processing · Desktop GUI

A MATLAB application that automates grading of multiple-choice answer sheets. It loads a scanned marking scheme and a student's sheet, uses image-processing operations to detect and count filled responses, compares them, and computes the score automatically — all through a GUIDE-built interface with load, mark, save, and reset controls.

  • Load marking scheme and student sheet as images
  • Automatic detection and comparison of marked responses
  • Score computation with results exported to a text file
  • Interactive GUI — load, mark, save, reset, exit
  • MATLAB
  • Image Processing Toolbox
  • GUIDE
  • Thresholding
  • Connected components

03

An Analysis of NearPeer's Influence on Student Enrollment

Northeastern University · ALY 6080 · Team of 4 · Python, pandas

A team analysis (with Obeng Adjei Paa Kwasi, Chinonso Ozoigbo, and David York) of whether engagement on the NearPeer student platform relates to university enrollment outcomes.

The notebook merges NearPeer user data with university datasets, then standardizes column names, resolves types and missing values, and joins on a shared identifier. Feature engineering adds time-based measures — days between deposit and September 1, and between last activity and September 1 — feeding an exploratory analysis of engagement patterns against enrollment.

  • pandas
  • NumPy
  • matplotlib
  • seaborn
  • pyjanitor
  • EDA
  • Data cleaning

Built on confidential NearPeer data, which is not redistributed.

04

Particle-Size Analysis with ImageJ & PyImageJ

Research assistantship · Python, PyImageJ / Fiji

Automated particle-size measurement for metal-powder samples — including Ti-6Al-4V (Ti64) and ball-milled powders — imaged by optical microscopy and FlowCam. A PyImageJ pipeline scripts Fiji from Python to batch-segment particles, extract per-particle measurements (area, equivalent diameter, circularity), and assemble size distributions.

Downstream, the measurements feed regression and visualization work comparing imaging and measurement methods across sample sets.

  • Headless Fiji scripting via PyImageJ from Python
  • Batch thresholding, watershed separation, Analyze Particles
  • Size-distribution statistics and cross-method regression
  • PyImageJ
  • Fiji / ImageJ
  • scikit-image
  • pandas
  • Regression
  • Materials imaging

Part of a collaborative research project; sample imagery and code are not public.

05

Spinal Abscess Prediction from ICU Records

Northeastern University · EAI 6010 · Team of 2 · Python, scikit-learn

A binary classifier (with Albert Darko, advised by Dr. Saeed Amal) that estimates whether an ICU patient will be diagnosed with a spinal abscess, built on the MIMIC-III critical-care database.

The cohort pairs spinal-abscess cases (ICD-9 codes beginning 324) with sampled controls, joining admissions, diagnoses, procedures, and patient tables. After cleaning, age derivation, and one-hot encoding, a logistic-regression model was evaluated with stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
